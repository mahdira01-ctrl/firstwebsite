Astronomy Club Cordialy Invites You To:

An exploration into all things space news!

Date: 10/22/26

Time: 7:00 PM CST

Location: Fayetteville Public Library

While the sun's up, we'll have complimentary food tables, presentations from actual astronomers, and a social hour. After the night sky awakens, we'll bust out the telescopes and look at stars, moons, planets, and every single other cool thing we can find. Feel free to bring your own telescopes too!

Our picture of the week is Hubble's latest released image. It's currently looking for a lensed supernova!

If you like free food and geeking out over space, you'll fit right in!
